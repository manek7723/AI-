Madhvi Enterprise 
Company Profile Content 
About the Legacy 
Founded in 1980, Madhvi Enterprise has built a four-decade legacy across mining, mineral 
logistics, industrial project execution, transaction structuring, and international commodity 
trade. The company’s journey reflects execution at scale, long-term trust with major 
industrial and institutional counterparties, deep mining expertise, and sustained leadership in 
the bauxite business. From large-scale infrastructure development and captive mine 
operations to government-linked mining assignments, strategic mineral transactions, 
regulatory mandates, Dalmia-linked operations, and long-term supply relationships with 
Chinese refineries and other international buyers, Madhvi Enterprise represents a rare 
combination of operational depth and commercial credibility in the mining sector. 
1. Tata Chemicals, Lalpur, Gujarat 
Around 1985, Madhvi Enterprise played a foundational role in the early-stage development 
of the Tata Chemicals project at Lalpur, Gujarat, during the establishment of the plant. Acting 
as the main contractor, Madhvi Enterprise executed the entire assigned scope of 
site-development work independently. The project included approximately 15 km of road 
construction, excavation of nearly 7,00,000 cubic meters, and development work across 
around 100 acres of land. 
This assignment was particularly significant because the site presented difficult terrain 
conditions due to the salty nature of the land, requiring strong on-ground execution capability 
and practical engineering judgment. Madhvi Enterprise successfully addressed these 
challenges, accelerated the construction-readiness of the site, and completed the work 
within the required deadline. This project stands as an early example of the firm’s large-scale 
earthwork and infrastructure execution capability. 
2. Carborundum Universal Limited, Mahadeva, 
Devbhumi Dwarka, Gujarat 
From approximately 1985 to 2005, Madhvi Enterprise served as the exclusive mining agency 
for Carborundum Universal Limited, a listed entity, for its captive bauxite mining operations in 
Mahadeva, Devbhumi Dwarka, Gujarat. 
This was a long-term captive mine arrangement under which Madhvi Enterprise handled the 
complete operational cycle for all of the client’s bauxite requirements from that belt. The 
monthly production scale was approximately 50,000 MT of ROM bauxite. The scope of 
operations included extraction, overburden removal, sizing, sorting, loading, dispatch, quality 
control, and full labour and equipment management. 
This engagement is one of the strongest demonstrations of Madhvi Enterprise’s mining 
capability and long-term reliability. The operations were carried out continuously for nearly 
two decades with no major disruption, while maintaining superior grade consistency and 
dependable supply to the client. The project was marked by scale ramp-up, high recovery, 
strong quality reputation, and long-term exclusivity, establishing Madhvi Enterprise as a 
trusted mining execution partner in Gujarat’s bauxite sector. 
3. GMDC, Kutch, Gujarat 
Between approximately 1985 and 2000, Madhvi Enterprise carried out bauxite mining 
operations directly for Gujarat Mineral Development Corporation at multiple mine locations in 
Kutch, including Ratadia, Daban, Roha, Kotla, and Naghrecha. 
The assignment involved monthly handling of around 35,000 MT of ROM bauxite. Madhvi 
Enterprise undertook the end-to-end operational scope including mining, sizing, sorting, 
loading, and transportation. The engagement was notable for its exclusivity in practice, with 
mining operations at the assigned locations being undertaken entirely by Madhvi Enterprise. 
This was a highly significant government-sector mining assignment and reflects the firm’s 
ability to operate under disciplined production expectations while maintaining continuity 
throughout varying operating conditions. Madhvi Enterprise consistently performed above 
targets and built a rare long-duration track record in GMDC-linked mining operations. The 
project remains an important marker of the company’s technical mining capability, execution 
discipline, and credibility in large institutional assignments. 
4. Essar Project, Wadinar, Jamnagar, Gujarat 
Around 1995, Madhvi Enterprise contributed extensively to the development of the Essar Oil 
Refinery ecosystem at Wadinar, Jamnagar, including works related to the refinery, port 
development, railway siding infrastructure, road construction, and large-scale excavation. 
The scope handled by Madhvi Enterprise covered end-to-end execution across multiple 
infrastructure packages. In railway-related development, this included corridor earthwork, 
excavation, formation preparation, embankment work, leveling, filling, track-bed related 
groundwork, haulage support, and associated civil enabling works required for railway siding 
readiness. In port development, the work included filling, bulk earth movement, leveling, land 
development, reclamation-related support, haulage, and associated site-preparation 
activities. The project also involved major road construction and excavation works as part of 
the broader industrial infrastructure build-out. 
Madhvi Enterprise executed multiple work fronts together at a large scale, handling 
approximately 20,00,000 cubic meters of earthwork and nearly 20 km of development 
stretch. The assignment demanded speed, continuity of operations, management of difficult 
site conditions, deployment of a large workforce, and sustained use of equipment fleets. The 
successful execution of this project reinforced the firm’s reputation for delivering high-volume 
industrial infrastructure work in mission-critical environments. 
5. Strategic Dealmaking in the Sale of Bombay Minerals 
to Ashapura Group, 2001 
In 2001, Madhvi Enterprise, through the leadership and market role of its founder, played a 
strategic role in the sale of Bombay Minerals to Ashapura Group in a transaction valued at 
approximately Rs. 100 crores. 
In this transaction, the role was not merely introductory. Madhvi Enterprise’s leadership 
acted as mandate holder, negotiator, and transaction structurer in relation to the full transfer 
of the mining lease. The deal is publicly documented and stands as a strong example of the 
group’s commercial influence and transaction-making ability within the mining industry. 
This milestone reflects more than brokerage. It highlights deep sector relationships, 
trust-based access, understanding of mining assets, and the ability to bring together parties 
in high-value mineral transactions. It remains an important part of the business legacy, 
underscoring reputation, network strength, and dealmaking capability in the mining sector. 
6. Mining Operations in Goa for Fomento 
Madhvi Enterprise also undertook mining-related operations in Goa for Fomento, covering 
iron ore and bauxite linked activities. These engagements further expanded the firm’s 
operational presence beyond Gujarat and reflected its ability to work within major mineral 
belts across India. 
The work involved mining execution support across core operational functions such as 
extraction, material handling, sorting, loading, dispatch coordination, and related mining 
support activities. This phase strengthened Madhvi Enterprise’s exposure to iron ore 
operations alongside its established bauxite expertise and added to its experience in 
handling mining assignments in commercially important mineral jurisdictions. 
The Goa operations contributed to the company’s broader mining profile by demonstrating 
geographic versatility, commodity diversification, and adaptability in working across different 
mining environments and client structures. 
7. International Mining and Mineral Operations in West 
Africa, 2005 to 2010 
Between 2005 and 2010, Madhvi Enterprise expanded its footprint internationally through 
mining and mineral business engagements in West Africa, including Sierra Leone, Liberia, 
and Ghana. These operations included bauxite and iron ore related activities, along with 
downstream domestic sale arrangements and large-volume mineral movement. 
During this period, the business was associated with high-volume mineral handling and 
trading activity, including engagements connected with companies such as Vimetco, and 
developed operating exposure in cross-border mining and mineral supply environments. At 
its peak, the scale of operations reached approximately 1,00,000 tonnes per month across 
the relevant engagements. 
These assignments helped Madhvi Enterprise build international experience in mineral 
sourcing, mining-linked commercial operations, and overseas network development. The 
West African phase of the business demonstrated the firm’s ability to operate beyond India, 
develop mineral relationships in challenging resource geographies, and participate in 
large-scale bauxite and iron ore value chains. 
8. Bauxite Exports to Chinese Refineries and 
International Buyers, 2015 to Present 
Since 2015, Madhvi Enterprise has been engaged in large-scale exports of bauxite from 
India, with a primary focus on long-term supply to Chinese refineries, while also serving 
customers in Kuwait and other Middle Eastern markets. The exports are undertaken directly 
through Madhvi Enterprise. 
The business has maintained a consistent export scale of approximately 1,00,000 MT per 
month, with cargoes primarily routed through Okha Port. The exported bauxite is non-plant 
grade material used in aluminium production, and sourcing is carried out from Gujarat-based 
mines and mineral networks. 
Over the years, Madhvi Enterprise has built a strong position in the export segment through 
consistency of volume, reliability of supply, quality management, efficient port logistics, and 
long-standing international relationships. The company’s standing in this market is supported 
by annual export volumes, port movement data, customer recognition, ranking indicators, 
and established industry reputation. 
Madhvi Enterprise has emerged as one of the prominent and consistently performing Indian 
exporters in the bauxite trade, with long-standing supply relationships across key 
international markets. 
9. Flagship Regulatory Mandate for Large-Scale Bauxite 
Production Clearance, Gujarat 
Between 2016 and 2019, Madhvi Enterprise was entrusted with a major regulatory and 
permissioning mandate for a Gujarat-based bauxite mining lease holder to secure 
large-scale production clearance at the mine level. The assignment covered end-to-end 
coordination and execution of the permission process required to unlock 28,00,000 MT of 
bauxite extraction over the engagement period. 
This mandate stands out as one of the most significant regulatory execution assignments in 
the company’s portfolio. It reflects Madhvi Enterprise’s practical strength in mining 
permissions, institutional coordination, documentation discipline, and long-standing 
familiarity with Gujarat’s bauxite regulatory environment. 
Scope of Mandate 
The assignment required Madhvi Enterprise to manage the full permissioning framework 
needed to support large-volume production clearance. This included regulatory mapping, 
preparation and review of technical and statutory documentation, inter-departmental 
coordination, and continuous follow-up across the approval chain. 
The work involved active engagement with multiple authorities and departments connected 
to mining, environment, pollution control, and revenue administration. Since such mandates 
depend on sequencing, timing, and procedural accuracy, the company’s role was not limited 
to document submission. Madhvi Enterprise worked across the full approval cycle to ensure 
that each stage moved forward in coordination with the next, without unnecessary delay or 
procedural gaps. 
Documentation and Regulatory Coordination 
A major part of the assignment involved preparing, structuring, and supporting the 
documentation required for a production clearance of this scale. This included mining-related 
planning documents, production schedules, environmental and compliance-linked records, 
and technical justifications required by the relevant authorities. 
Madhvi Enterprise also played a key coordination role between departments, ensuring 
continuity in file movement, clarity in submissions, and responsiveness during the review 
process. The success of the mandate depended on disciplined follow-up and practical 
understanding of how large mining permissions are processed in the real regulatory 
environment. 
Outcome and Commercial Impact 
Through this engagement, production clearance for 28,00,000 MT of bauxite was secured, 
converting a large in-ground reserve into commercially actionable output for the lease holder. 
The mandate helped unlock substantial production capacity and created direct economic 
value for the client by enabling the reserve to move toward active extraction and marketable 
supply. 
The assignment also had wider sectoral relevance. Large-scale production approval of this 
nature supports royalty generation, state-level mineral activity, downstream industrial supply, 
and broader commercial movement across transport, port handling, and mineral trade 
channels. 
Strategic Value 
This project is an important example of Madhvi Enterprise’s capability beyond mining and 
export operations alone. It demonstrates the company’s ability to work effectively in the 
regulatory layer of the mining sector, especially where outcomes depend on institutional 
coordination, procedural accuracy, and long-term sector understanding. 
Project Snapshot 
● Permission volume secured: 28,00,000 MT 
● Engagement period: 2016 to 2019 
● Nature of work: Mine-level production permission and regulatory coordination 
● Core strengths demonstrated: Documentation, regulatory execution, 
inter-departmental coordination, mining-sector understanding 
● Commercial significance: Large reserve base activated for production 
10. Dalmia Operations, Gujarat to China 
Over a one-year operating period, Madhvi Enterprise executed a full-cycle bauxite sourcing 
and export operation linked to Dalmia-origin material, managing the business from 
mine-level procurement to overseas delivery. The company handled approximately 2,00,000 
tons of bauxite during this period, with regular monthly shipments to Chinese refineries for 
alumina extraction. This operation reflected Madhvi Enterprise’s strength in mining-linked 
trade execution, bulk logistics, export handling, and financial management. 
Mining and Source Execution 
Madhvi Enterprise managed the sourcing of bauxite from the mine location linked to Dalmia 
operations, ensuring steady material availability for export commitments. The company 
aligned mine-origin movement with shipment schedules and maintained continuity of 
dispatch throughout the operating cycle. This required disciplined planning at source so that 
cargo movement remained smooth and export volumes could be sustained without 
interruption. 
Logistics and Material Movement 
Madhvi Enterprise handled the logistics chain required to move bulk bauxite from the mining 
side into the export stream in an organized and dependable manner. This included planning 
the movement of material from source to dispatch points, coordinating bulk handling, and 
maintaining a regular cargo flow in line with vessel scheduling. Since the operation was 
based on one shipment per month, logistics had to be managed with strong discipline so that 
each cargo moved on time and in line with export commitments. 
Financial Management and Trade Discipline 
A key strength of Madhvi Enterprise in this operation was financial coordination across the 
full business cycle. Bulk mineral exports require careful control over procurement funding, 
cargo movement, working capital rotation, and payment realization. In this Dalmia-linked 
operation, Madhvi Enterprise managed these financial aspects in a way that supported 
regular monthly shipments and stable execution over the full period. 
Project Snapshot 
● Material handled: Approx. 2,00,000 tons 
● Operational period: 1 year 
● Export frequency: 1 shipment per month 
● End market: Chinese refineries 
● End use: Bauxite supplied for alumina extraction
